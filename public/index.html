ABOUT FBI GH

One company. Several connected capabilities.

FBI GH is positioned as a Ghana-based technology and media business serving organisations, events, homes and businesses with practical technical systems and creative production.

Our work spans computer networking, CCTV installation, smart homes and offices, electric fencing, equipment and data services, live production, livestreaming, photography, videography and related digital operations.

The new website is also the front door to the company's internal and client-facing cloud systems.
