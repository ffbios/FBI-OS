ABOUT FILM BEYOND IMAGINATION

One company. Several connected capabilities.

Film Beyond Imagination is positioned as a Ghana-based technology and media business serving organisations, events, homes and businesses with practical technical systems and creative production.

Our work spans photography, videography, live production, livestreaming, networking, CCTV installation, smart homes and offices, electric fencing, technical deployment and the professional management of artists and actors.

The new website is also the front door to the company's internal and client-facing cloud systems.
