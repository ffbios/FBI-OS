Who we are

Creative thinking. Serious delivery.

Film Beyond Imagination is a Ghana-based creative and technology company. We combine production, broadcast and connected technology to help businesses, communities and individuals communicate and operate better.

From film production and live programmes to CCTV deployment and communication campaigns, we focus on planning, dependable delivery and work we can stand behind.

Three divisions · one trusted team

One team for the whole picture.

FBI Media

Film and video production, photography, post-production and aerial cinematography.

FBI Live

Live event production, broadcast and original programmes for audiences in Ghana and beyond.

FBI Technology & Connect

CCTV deployment, connected technology and communication campaigns for practical everyday needs.
