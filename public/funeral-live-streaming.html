FBI Live · Farewell media

No family member left out of the farewell.

Many families have loved ones abroad, elderly relatives far from Accra, or friends who simply cannot travel. FBI streams funerals, memorial services and burials live in clear HD on a private link, so everyone who loved the deceased can be present.

Our crew is respectful and discreet. We arrive early to set up and test, and bring backup internet so the stream stays up from the first hymn to the final committal.
